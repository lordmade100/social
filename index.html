How It Works
1
PDF Parsing — Bank statement is parsed with PDF.js. Transactions, balances, and keywords are extracted.
2
Feature Calculation — 8 normalised risk features are computed: volatility, balance trend, debit/credit ratio, reversal frequency, etc.
3
Grok AI Analysis — Features + applicant context are sent to the Grok API, which reasons over the data, estimates default probability, and provides a detailed explanation.
4
Decision + Storage — Result (APPROVE / REVIEW / REJECT) and AI reasoning are saved to Firebase for your portfolio.
